Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026 · Operator: Tui đầu tư

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the automated content publishing tool operated by Tui đầu tư for the "Tui dau tu" channel (the "Service", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, stored, secured, and how you can exercise control over it, including in connection with the Service's use of third-party publishing APIs such as TikTok's Content Posting API, Meta's Graph API, and the YouTube Data API.

1. Who We Are

The Service is operated by Tui đầu tư ("we", "us", "our"), the developer and operator of the TikTok app "Tui dau tu" and the associated content-publishing automation described in these documents. You can reach us using the contact details in Section 10.

2. Data We Collect

When an account owner connects a platform account to the Service, we access and store only:

  • An OAuth access token and refresh token issued by the platform (e.g. TikTok, YouTube, Facebook) after the account owner explicitly authorizes the connection.
  • A platform-issued user identifier (e.g. TikTok's open_id), used only to address API calls to the correct account.
  • Basic account/creator information required by the platform's publishing API (e.g. display name, avatar URL, and available privacy-level/posting options returned by the platform's creator_info endpoint).
  • Metadata about videos submitted for publishing (title/caption, privacy level chosen, and the publish status/ID returned by the platform).

We do not request or access any TikTok user data beyond what is strictly required to publish video content to the connected account (scopes user.info.basic, video.publish, and video.upload). We do not access followers' data, direct messages, or any content the account owner has not explicitly submitted for publishing.

3. How We Use Data

Access tokens are used solely to authenticate API requests that publish video content to the account owner's own connected account. Basic profile information (display name/avatar) is used only to confirm which account is connected and to populate the publishing request. No data collected through these APIs is sold, shared with advertisers, used for profiling, or used for any purpose other than operating the Service as described in this Policy.

4. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

Access tokens, refresh tokens, and the minimal account metadata above are stored on infrastructure controlled by the operator (private, access-restricted storage; not a public database) and are transmitted only to the platform that issued them (to refresh or use the token) and never to any other third party. Tokens and associated metadata are retained only for as long as the Service continues publishing to the connected account, and are permanently deleted:

  • Immediately upon the account owner revoking access from the platform's own settings (see Section 5); or
  • Within 30 days of a written deletion request sent to the contact address in Section 10; or
  • Automatically once a refresh token expires and is not renewed.

5. Revoking Access

The account owner can revoke the Service's access to any connected platform at any time from that platform's own account/app-permissions settings (for example, TikTok's "Manage apps" page under Settings > Manage account > Third party apps, or Facebook's "Business Integrations" settings). Once access is revoked, the corresponding locally stored tokens are deleted and no further API calls are made on behalf of that account.

6. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored tokens and metadata, including access-restricted storage, encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) for all API calls, and limiting access to the minimum systems/personnel necessary to operate the Service. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for use by adult account owners managing their own social media accounts and is not directed at, marketed to, or knowingly used to collect personal data from children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in their jurisdiction).

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by us, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to receive a copy of your data in a portable format. You can exercise any of these rights by contacting us using the details in Section 10; we will respond within a reasonable time and no later than 30 days.

9. Third-Party Services

The Service interacts with third-party platform APIs (TikTok, Meta/ Facebook, YouTube/Google) solely to publish content, in compliance with each platform's developer terms and policies (including TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy). Each platform's own privacy policy governs its handling of any data processed on its own servers, independent of this Policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Service or applicable law. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
